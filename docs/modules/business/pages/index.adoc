= Business guide
:description: What the shop does for customers and the back office, in business terms.

This part describes the shop as a business process: what a customer can do, what the back office
does, and the rules that hold no matter which service is involved.

The shop is a *single seller*: one company sells its own goods. A marketplace, where many sellers
offer products and are paid out separately, is out of scope for now and listed as an optional
sprint on the xref:ROOT:roadmap.adoc[roadmap]. Technical details are in the
xref:architecture:index.adoc[architecture] and xref:developer:index.adoc[developer] guides.

== Who does what

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Actor |Can

|Customer
|Browse the catalog without logging in; create a profile with a shipping address; place orders;
see and cancel their own orders.

|Back office (admin)
|Create products and change prices; take products off sale; book goods receipts; see and dispatch or
deliver shipments; see any order; deactivate customers.

|Warehouse
|Hands parcels to the carrier (dispatch) and confirms delivery, through the back-office functions.
|===
