[cols="1,3,2"]
|===
|State |Meaning |Customer can cancel?

|Placed
|The order is stored with the prices of that moment; stock is being reserved, then the amount held
on the card.
|Yes, immediately.

|Confirmed
|Stock is reserved and the amount held on the card; a parcel is being prepared.
|Yes, as a request: the answer follows shortly.

|Cancellation requested
|The warehouse is checking whether the parcel can still be stopped.
|Already requested.

|Shipped
|The parcel is with the carrier; the stock has left the warehouse and the money is taken.
|No. Later: a return.

|Delivered
|The customer has the goods.
|No. Later: a return.

|Cancelled
|Final. Any reserved stock is available again, and any hold on the card is released.
|—
|===

== Why cancelling a confirmed order is a request

Once a parcel is prepared, only the warehouse knows whether it has left. If the shop cancelled on its
own, an order could be cancelled and refunded while the parcel is on its way. So the shop asks, and
the warehouse answers: stopped, or already gone. Either answer leaves stock, order and parcel
in agreement.

== When the customer pays

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Moment |Money

|Order confirmed
|The amount is *held* on the card (authorized). Nothing is taken yet.

|Parcel handed to the carrier
|The money is *taken* (captured). Customers pay for goods that are on their way, not for promises.

|Order cancelled before that
|The hold is *released* (voided). The customer was never charged.

|Card declined
|The order is cancelled with "Payment declined", and the stock is free again.

|Provider does not answer
|The order waits as Placed: not declined, not charged twice. After 30 minutes without an answer it is
cancelled, and any hold released.
|===

If the money cannot be taken when the parcel leaves (for example, the hold expired), the parcel is
not called back: the order stays Shipped and the payment becomes *overdue*, for the back office to
collect. A refund after a return follows with returns (s09).

== Returns

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Return is |Meaning

|Requested
|The customer asked to send units back, with a reason, within 14 days of delivery. Nothing is refunded yet.

|Received
|The parcel is back at the warehouse: the units are stock again, and the refund has started.

|Refunded
|The money for the returned units is back with the customer, at the prices paid.

|Rejected
|The back office refused it, for example because the parcel never arrived. The units can be returned in a new request.

|Refund failed
|The units are back, but the money could not be returned (for example, the card account was closed). The back office takes over.
|===

An order can have several returns, together never more units than were bought. The order itself
stays Delivered.
