= Domain rules
:description: The business rules every service enforces, whatever the technical path.

[cols="1,3"]
|===
|Topic |Rule

|Prices
|The price of an order line is the catalog price at the moment of placing. The customer's request
cannot set a price.

|Stock
|An order reserves all its lines or none. Reserved units cannot be sold twice; they leave stock when
the parcel is handed to the carrier, and become available again when the order is cancelled.

|Placing twice
|A customer who sends the same order twice (a double click, a retry after a timeout) gets one order.
The repeated request returns the same order.

|Cancellation
|A placed order is cancelled at once. A confirmed order is cancelled only if its parcel has not left;
the answer comes from the warehouse. A shipped order cannot be cancelled.

|Order expiry
|An order that is not confirmed within 30 minutes is cancelled ("Not confirmed in time"). A confirmed
order not handed to the carrier within 3 days is cancelled if the parcel can still be stopped
("Not dispatched in time"). Both times are configurable.

|Payment
|Every order names a payment method. The order is confirmed only when its stock is reserved *and*
its amount is held on the card. The money is taken when the parcel is handed to the carrier, never
before; a cancelled order holds no money. A customer is never charged twice for one order, even
when the payment provider answers late or not at all. A provider that does not answer has not
declined.

|Money that cannot be taken
|If the money cannot be taken after the parcel has left, the parcel is not called back: the order
stays shipped and the payment is overdue.

|Delivery
|An order can only be delivered after it was shipped.

|Products off sale
|A deactivated product cannot be ordered any more. Orders placed before keep their reservation and
complete normally.

|Inactive customers
|A deactivated customer cannot place new orders.

|Privacy of orders
|A customer only sees their own orders. Someone else's order looks exactly like one that does not
exist.
|===
