[NOTE]
.Facts to build on (remove when the story is written)
====
* Before s06, order-service cancelled on its own and told the others.
* If the dispatch happened in the same moment: the order was CANCELLED, catalog released the stock, the parcel left anyway, and the dispatch event ended in a dead-letter topic.
* Fix: a confirmed order only asks (CANCELLATION_REQUESTED); shipping, which owns the point of no return, answers shipment-cancelled or cancellation-refused.
* Inside shipping, dispatch and cancellation of one shipment are serialised by its version (optimistic locking); the loser is retried and sees the winner's outcome.
* Every interleaving ends cancelled with stock released, or shipped with stock committed.
====
