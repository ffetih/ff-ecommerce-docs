== Health and endpoints

Every service exposes `/actuator/health` (open, for probes), `/actuator/prometheus` (role
`monitoring` or `admin`, for Prometheus) and `/actuator/metrics` (role `admin`). The gateway does not
route `/actuator`; call each service on its own port. The gateway's own actuator is on its management
port, 8089.
